Live demo

ALIKED, RaCo or XFeat keypoints and descriptors for two images, computed in this page on your GPU through WebGPU, and the matches between them. Nothing is uploaded: your images stay in the browser. The matcher (mutual nearest neighbours with a ratio test) is part of this demo, not of the library. Use the library in your page.

Keypoints

Detector

ALIKED detects and describes keypoints in one network.

Selection

RaCo's ranker scores which keypoints are worth keeping when you need fewer; its authors subsample by them rather than by detection score. All: keep every detected keypoint.

Precision

FP32 matches the original network; FP16 is faster, with about 99.8 % of the keypoints the same.

Backend

The CPU backend runs in WebAssembly on one thread: about a second per 640x480 image.

Matches and display

Keep a match when its distance is below this fraction of the second best. 1 keeps every mutual nearest neighbour.

RaCo's keypoint uncertainty is typically about a pixel, too small to see at true size; larger multiples show which keypoints are the uncertain ones.

Run again for warm timings: the first run on a new setting compiles the GPU shaders.
